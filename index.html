Why we built this

VELOCITY exists for one reason: supercars are rolling arguments about what matters — grip over comfort, grams over convenience, sound over silence. We catalogue them the way a horologist catalogues movements: obsessively, by the numbers, without apology.

Every specification on this page is presented as published by each manufacturer at launch. Consider it a living reference, not a showroom — built for the 2 a.m. rabbit hole, not the sales pitch.
